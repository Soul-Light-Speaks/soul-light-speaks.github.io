The one who loved you most
is no longer here.
No amount of noise
can fill the emptiness within.

When your parents are gone,
life becomes a one-way road—
with no way back.
No matter how loud the laughter,
it can never bring back
your parents’ presence.

A life filled with children and grandchildren
may seem joyful and complete.
Yet beneath all that noise,
there is often a quiet emptiness—
because the one who loved you most
is no longer here.

Parents are the strongest shelter you will ever have.
In storms, they stand before you.
In sunlight, they share the warmth with you.

As long as your father is there,
you still have a sky to lean on.
As long as your mother is there,
you still have a place called home.

But one day,
when the two people who gave you life are gone,
there is no one left in this world
who will ever love you the same way—
without asking for anything in return.

No one will quietly care about your joys and sorrows,
accept your flaws,
or put up with your stubbornness like they did.

All that remains
are memories… and tears.

When your parents are still here,
life has a place to return to.

When they are gone,
life becomes a one-way journey.

No love can replace the love of parents.
No matter how many smiles surround you,
it cannot fill the longing
to be held…
to have someone to lean on.

We walk with our parents
to the later stages of their lives.

We see them surrounded by family,
yet still carrying a quiet sense of loneliness—
because their own parents
have long been gone.

Perhaps this is life’s deepest paradox:
the more we have,
the more we feel what is missing.

And this emptiness reminds us—
while we still can love,
do not hold back your hugs.

Treasure this moment.
Treasure each other.
So that no regrets are left behind.
