中文 | English | 日本語
That encounter
was never meant to stay—
only to light
a part of your path.

That light
belongs to the night,
and to memory.

That quiet, unreachable glow
never truly fades.

Some people
are just passing scenes
in your life.

You see that person, your heart races,
but you cannot stay.

The one you could never have
was never meant
to walk your whole life with you.

That person appears only
to light a part of your path.

That light came too early,
too bright.

It lit a part of your journey,
and the longing within you.

Years later, looking back,
you realize—
that light was not perfect.

You just needed it
so much back then.

If you missed it,
do not be sad.

It was just a lesson
life arranged,

reminding you:
you loved sincerely
and gave your all.

If you had it,
do not cling.

Maybe that love came
only to repay a past love.

When It is done,
everything turns and leaves.

What is missed
does not need to be chased.

What once came
does not need to be held.

Some lights
are meant to be seen
from a distance.

Not yours to own,
only to remind you—
how deeply you once loved.

The one who stays
is not always
the one who amazed your youth.

It is the one willing
to grow with you.

When debts of love are cleared,
when both choose to grow,
the relationship transforms—
from infatuation
to something deeper.

Not being able to forget
does not mean
you should go back.

The one you could never have
is not meant to be forgotten,

but to prove—
you once loved
with all your heart.

That presence may seem perfect,
almost unreal.

But the real brilliance
was you—
the one whose heart
raced for someone.

The one you could never have
is the light left by youth.

It will not disappear,
but it was never yours to keep.

It only reminds you—
you once loved
purely and completely.

Let it stay in memory,
and it will remain perfect.

One day, you will meet
someone whose presence
does not shake your heart like light,

but steadies it.

That steadiness
outshines any brilliance.

Then you will understand—
the one who truly stays
does not make you lose control,
but brings you peace.

Even now, when you see that person,
your heart may still race.

That is a trace of youth.
A quiet reminder—
you once truly lived,
and truly loved.

The one you could never have
is the ember of youth,

reminding you
how sincere you once were.
