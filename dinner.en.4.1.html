Childhood—
being stood up
felt unfair, painful.

Growing up—
broken promises
sometimes unavoidable,
choices of adulthood.

As a child,
being stood up by adults
felt unfair and painful.

Growing up,
I realized breaking promises
is common.

Adults carry countless responsibilities.
They have lives to manage—
for work, for making a living, for clients,
or even just to stay afloat.

Those who truly care about you
will make the effort to show up.

Broken promises are no longer betrayals;
they are reminders of reality.

The most precious are those
who, even when busy,
still choose to be by your side.
