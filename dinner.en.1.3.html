中文 | English | 日本語
Some stay for a night—
yet are never forgotten.

Some stay a lifetime,
yet never truly grow close.

We have all,
at some point,
been travelers
in each other’s lives.

Some souls
come into this world
just to visit.

A few hours,
a few days—
or a few short years.

Like a passing breeze,
they quietly leave.

Yet for the parents
who welcomed them,
they are never forgotten.

They leave behind
soft creases on the sheets,
and a pillow
no one dares to replace.

Some call it
a short life.

But in their parents’ hearts,
they are a guest
who never truly leaves.

Some choose to stay
for a lifetime.

Fifty years
under the same roof—

yet they feel
like strangers next door.

A nod,
a passing greeting.

Doors remain closed.
Words grow fewer.

They move through time
side by side—

but never truly together.

Love is still there,
just buried
beneath too much silence.

Some stay for a night
and are gone—

yet remain unforgettable.

Some stay a lifetime,
yet never learn
how to be close.

Most of us
are only passing through
each other’s lives.

A meal.
A few words.
A single embrace.

A brief crossing—
that quietly changes a life.

Life is like
a quiet inn for the soul.

Some leave laughter.
Some leave tears.

Some just pass by.
Some choose to stay.

No matter who leaves first,
every meeting
leaves a trace of light.

Every soul
has been a guest
in someone’s life—

and once,
a light
in someone’s heart.
