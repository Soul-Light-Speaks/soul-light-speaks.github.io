The journey of the soul:

Beginning—
to feel pain
and to cause it.

Middle—
to face desire
and inner darkness.

End—
to choose discipline
and let go of harm.

To let go
is to release attachment,
to purify the heart,
and to walk toward freedom.

Life is a journey of lessons.
We come into this world to experience them all.

If we cause harm in this life,
we may return in another life
to feel the same pain we once gave.

And in the life after that,
we may walk a path of repentance and growth.

If life feels difficult now,
hold on to wisdom—
it may be an echo of past actions,
allowing us to understand
the suffering we once caused.

The difference between the awakened and the unawakened
lies in self-restraint.

Those who are not yet awakened
still move between good and evil.

But those who are awakened
understand the law of cause and effect.

They do not refrain from harm out of fear alone,
but from a deeper knowing—
that every harmful act
returns as suffering.

And so, they choose not to harm.

The soul’s journey through reincarnation
can be understood in three stages:

First,
to experience both causing pain and receiving it,
and to understand its consequences.

Second,
to face the trials of desire, greed, and darkness,
and to recognize human weakness.

Third,
to choose discipline and compassion,
to let go of harmful patterns,
and to move toward awakening and inner clarity.

True self-restraint begins within.

It is the quiet decision
not to repeat the past.

To release desire,
to loosen attachment,
to purify the heart—

and to slowly step
into true freedom.
