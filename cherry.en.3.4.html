Forgetting is a gift.
Remembering past lives
may reveal lessons still unfinished.

Memories rise quietly,
leading you back
to that familiar presence.

Remembering past lives
is not always beautiful.

Fragments of joy and pain
quietly shape
the choices we make today.

Each life
is a new lesson,
a new path to walk.

Memories of the past
are only reminders—
guiding the soul
toward wholeness.

Sometimes,
the soul lets you remember
only a part.

Perhaps
it is an unfinished lesson,
or something meant
to continue.

Or perhaps,
you are here
to help renew this world.

If everything were remembered,
it would not always be a blessing.

The pain, the tears,
the lessons long completed—

when they return,
they may bring
a quiet sense of release,
or a gentle smile.

But they could also
hold you
in the darkness of the past.

Memories never surface
without reason.

They arrive
when the soul is ready,
guiding us
toward certain encounters.

In this life,
we meet familiar souls again.

Even with different faces,
even in new roles—

something deep within
still recognizes them.

As if a quiet voice whispers:
every meeting
has its reason.
