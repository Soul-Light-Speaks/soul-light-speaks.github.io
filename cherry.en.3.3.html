This is not suffering.
It is not abandonment or punishment.
It is a soul choosing
to walk through the dark—
to remember that light begins within.

Some souls
choose a life with no support.

Not because they deserve suffering,
but because they are ready
to learn how to light their own path
through the dark.

When no one understands you
and no one is there to hold you up,
it does not mean you are not enough.
It means your soul is walking through
a deeper trial.

Remember this:
the dark is not here to swallow you.
It is here to remind you
that you were always meant to shine.
