The stage lights rise.
We step into different roles.

A spark of recognition—
a fleeting echo of something familiar.

When the curtain falls,
our eyes meet,
and a knowing smile passes between us.

Past Lives & This Life (Within the Script)

The lights come on again in another lifetime.
You and I step into new costumes,
wearing unfamiliar faces and voices.

We cross paths once more in the crowd,
yet remain strangers in the story.

Perhaps something stirs deep inside—
a fleeting sense of déjà vu,
like a silhouette we almost recognize,
but cannot name.

And so, we pass each other again,
leaving behind unspoken words
and an unfinished bond.

Final Curtain (The Soul Returns)

The curtain falls.
The story ends.
The lights slowly fade.

We lay down our roles,
step out of our costumes,
and return to who we truly are.

Then you walk toward me—
softly calling a name
long forgotten in this world.

We smile at each other,
like actors after the show,
finally speaking from the heart.

We talk about the scenes we shared—
who cried, who laughed,
who loved, and who let go.

All the silent emotions
find their voice at last,
held and understood beyond words.

Life cannot be replayed,
and the meetings on stage will fade.

But when souls recognize each other,
it happens after the curtain falls—
and again,
when it rises once more.

The words left unsaid
will return in that reunion,
echoing as something eternal between us.

✨ Closing

Life is a stage.
Every meeting is part of an unfinished script.
Even if we pass each other in different roles,
our souls will always recognize each other
after the curtain falls.
