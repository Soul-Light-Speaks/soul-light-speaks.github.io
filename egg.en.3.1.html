Death is not the end.
The light still stretches ahead.
In a single thought, you find peace.

Death is not the end.
It is a return to the soul’s true self,
a moment when you begin to remember who you really are.
Fear and calm are only a door apart.

The soul never truly dies.
It simply awakens from a dream.
