I did not give you the answer.
I left you with curiosity
when you woke up.

And that curiosity
made you start walking.

I did not give you a direction.
I only left a small light within your heart.

That light is not big,
but it awakens a quiet longing inside you.

Not the desire to walk the “right” path,
but the courage to walk in your own way.

Some answers
are not meant to be understood right away.

They are meant to grow within you
as you continue your journey.

You do not need to see the whole road at once.
Just keep taking the next step.

Every step you take
is an answer you find for yourself.
