Not everything
that leaves a mark
is meant to be forgotten.
Some things
are meant to travel
with you through life.

Should you forget
someone you once loved deeply
after they are gone?

In truth,
whether you remember
or let go,

both are simply
part of the soul’s journey.

Time does not always erase everything,
but it softens the weight—
until one day,
you can finally breathe with it.

Some things
run too deep
to be forgotten.

They are not meant to disappear,
but to be carried with you
as you go on living.
