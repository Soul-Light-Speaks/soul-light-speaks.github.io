Love teaches lessons—
sometimes while together,
sometimes after loss.
No matter when,
your lesson is complete.

Love is not always about
staying with the same person.

Some connections reveal their meaning
while you are still together.
Others only make sense
after You have gone your separate ways.

Maybe You have met someone like this:

When you were together,
you were the one giving everything—
while they only kept receiving.

And only after you chose to leave
did they finally realize your worth,
filled with regret, wanting you back.

But for you,
the lesson was already complete.
There was nothing left to continue.

Some people only learn how to love
after they lose you.
That is their lesson.

Others never truly loved at all—
they were only enjoying what you gave,
and left without a ripple.

People like this…
may simply be part of your lesson in life.

They were not meant to love you deeply,
but to show you—through distance and indifference—
what love truly is,
and how to bring it back to yourself.

Not every love story
is meant to be mutual
or equally felt.

Some people awaken through loss.
Some pass through your life like the wind.

But no matter which it is—

if You have learned what you needed,
then the connection
was never meaningless.
