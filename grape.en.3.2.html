Doing good deeds without expecting anything in return,
often brings unexpected turns of beauty
into your life.

In many stories, books, and even real-life accounts,
people often speak of—
a kind-hearted person walking down the street,
encounters an old man or an old woman.

The old man or woman might casually ask:
“Young one, could you help me carry this luggage?”
“Do you want to walk with me to the temple?”

So, they take a slightly different step,
change their path,
and avoid an accident.

Or a stall owner might choose not to sell a flawed item,
turning instead to grab a new one—
just as the ceiling collapses where he had been standing.

Not because he wanted anything,
just because he stayed true to his conscience.

Some believe that those elderly figures are not ordinary—
maybe manifestations of deities,
or silent guides reminding us along the way.

But perhaps more important than who they are,
is the kind of person you choose to be in that moment.

These little turns in life
are often not miracles,
but protections drawn by good intentions,
quietly steering people away from harm.

Doing good deeds
is not for reward,
but to subtly open another path for yourself in the world.
