Letting go
does not mean
you do not love.

It means
You have learned
to let life
unfold.

Sometimes, the highest form of love
is not protection,
but trust.

Letting go
is an act of trust—
and a kind of faith.

Parents often love too deeply—
so deeply
they fear their children getting hurt,
or taking the wrong path.

They remind them, again and again:
“Slow down. Do not rush. Stay safe.”

But a child’s heart
longs for the wind.

They want to move faster,
to feel the pulse of freedom—

even if that freedom
comes with falls
and scars.

Perhaps, in the end,
parents must learn
to let go—

to allow life
to unfold as it is.

Life is both a script
and a test.

Each of us
has our own questions to answer,
our own pitfalls to step into.

Growth is not about avoiding mistakes,
but becoming who you are
through them.
