Do not wait
for the funeral.
Show your love.

Do not wait until the funeral
to do what should have been done.

What some people deeply long for while they are still alive
is not something material—
It is to be understood,
to be heard,
to be truly seen.

And yet, so often,
we only realize our regret
and try to make up for it
after they are gone.

We bring our best to the funeral—
but by then,
they can no longer hear us.

If you can,
be there while they are still alive.
Stay close. Talk. Listen.

It is not about indulgence—
It is about understanding.

Do not let
“making up at the funeral”
turn into a lifelong regret.

💔 Love them while they are still here
💔 Listen more, blame less
💔 Do not wait until It is too late
