中文 | English | 日本語
Fortune-telling shows
the path you were meant to take.

Your thoughts and choices
create a different path.

It may seem off—
but You have already
changed your future.

Fortune-telling is not wrong.
It simply shows the path
you were most likely to take,
based on your current energy.

It reflects the main road—
your natural direction,
your original path.

But now,
through your thoughts and choices,
You have stepped onto a different path.

And so,
the future begins to shift.

It may seem like the reading was wrong,
but in truth,
You have already changed your path.

Fortune-telling showed
what could have been.

Your free will
has created a different future.
