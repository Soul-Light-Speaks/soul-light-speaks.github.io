Through the years,
we have quietly said goodbye
to many souls for the last time.

Meeting fully,
even for a moment,
is already a blessing.

If fate allows,
we will meet again.

We have, without noticing,
already said goodbye
to many people for the last time.

Perhaps a teacher or classmate,
perhaps a mentor or colleague.
That casual “goodbye”
sometimes becomes a silent full stop.

When we were young, we thought
people would always stay.
Leaving felt like betrayal;
losing felt like being left behind.

As we grow, we realize—
not every departure carries regret.
Some turns simply guide us
toward paths better suited for our journey.

To walk together for a while is fate;
to live gently in each other’s memory is a blessing.

No need to cling to “we must meet again.”
Be fully present
in every encounter,
with an open heart.

Grateful for this moment.
If fate allows,
we will meet again.
