In the passing crowd,
our eyes met.

We smiled,
a small wave.

And just like that,
we were gone again—
lost in the crowd.

In a sea of strangers,
I caught a glimpse of someone familiar.

For a moment,
time seemed to stand still.

We did not say much—
just a smile, a small wave.

Some people
no longer belong in our lives,
yet remain,
quietly, gently.

Some goodbyes
do not need to be planned.

In the crowd,
she recognized me at a glance.

That smile seemed to say,
“We have both been doing okay, have not we?”

In that moment,
the world felt quiet.

We both smiled,
saying nothing at all.

Then the crowd carried us away,
back to our separate paths.

But somewhere in my heart,
that familiar silhouette
still lingers.
