中文 | English | 日本語
Some people
never have children.

Yet through love
and quiet kindness,
they move the heavens.

And miracles
find their way.

Pregnancy
is not the end.

Only kindness
makes blessings last

Heaven is compassionate.
Kindness never fades.

There are people in this world
who go through life without children.

Yet, by holding kindness in their hearts
and doing good for others,
they are still blessed by Heaven.

Some adopt a child,
and later, a new life arrives unexpectedly.

This is grace unfolding—
Heaven opening the door to a miracle.

But remember:
pregnancy is not the end of goodness.

If compassion fades after receiving a child,
those blessings may fade as well.

Heaven’s gifts
are often given in advance.

Only those who continue to do good,
to cultivate virtue,
and to care for all living beings—

can truly hold on to
this heaven-given blessing.
