You are not alone.
There is always a light
that will open the door for you.
And now,you have learned how to
open the door with your own light.

You are not alone.
There is a light that comes quietly,
opening doors when you least expect it.

On my first day in a foreign land,
I stood outside the university transit station with two heavy suitcases.
Someone walked up without a word and held the door for me.

On my last day before leaving,
I found myself standing before the same door again.
The same place. The same door. The same person.
He opened it for me once more.

It felt strangely beautiful—like a silent understanding,
like something gently arranged by fate.

Between the beginning and the end,
a light had quietly walked beside me in a full circle.

And in that moment, I understood.I was never alone.
The light had always been there—
only now, I could finally see it.
