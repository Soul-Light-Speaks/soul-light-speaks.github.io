Chant with sincerity,
lost souls find their way.

Do good without seeking reward,
you uplift others and yourself.

Make vows with compassion,
your light reaches all beings.

Doing good deeds
helps the people who are still living.

Reciting prayers
comforts the souls that are still wandering in darkness.

But there is another kind of help.

The quiet kind.
The kind no one sees.

You are not weak.

You simply have not believed in your own light yet.

🌿 A sincere intention never needs proof or rank.
🕯 True sincerity is the strongest energy there is.

When you sit down quietly
and begin to recite your prayers,

you might think
you are only calming your own heart.

But in that moment,

somewhere beyond what you can see,
a soul may be touched by your light.

They draw closer.
They listen.
They weep.

You may never know.

But they remember.

A single sincere thought from you
can become a doorway to light for someone else.

And when you make a quiet vow in your heart—

that your kindness and prayers
are not for yourself,

but for more souls to find light,
and for this world to become a little warmer—

that is compassion.

And compassion
is a force even the unseen world responds to.
